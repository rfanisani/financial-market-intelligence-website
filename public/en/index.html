Calculation method

Implied USD is a calculated rate derived from the ratio of domestic gold prices to global gold prices. It is not an official or tradable rate.

Implied USD = domestic price ÷ (weight × purity × global gold per gram) — global gram = ounce ÷ 31.1034768
