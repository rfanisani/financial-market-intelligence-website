About Market Radar

Market Radar is a lightweight, no-clutter site for key global and Iranian market prices. No accounts, no ads, no tracking — the whole site is under one megabyte.

Data is automatically fetched from public sources, validated and published as static files roughly every 4 hours. Each value shows its source and update time. If a fetch fails, the last known good data is kept.

Implied USD is a calculated rate: the ratio of domestic gold prices to the USD value of global gold, adjusted for purity and weight. It can differ significantly from official or free-market rates.
