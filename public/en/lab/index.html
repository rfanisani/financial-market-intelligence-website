Financial Intelligence Lab

Four lightweight tools for analysing the daily price history published on Market Radar. All calculations run in your browser — nothing is stored or sent.

Data status: Fresh · Last update: 17 Oct 2026, 09:56

Choose a tool and an asset, then press Calculate.

Calculation methods

Total return = (last − first) ÷ first × 100

Annualised volatility = std. dev. of daily returns × √252

Max drawdown = largest peak-to-trough decline within the window

Moving average = simple mean of the last n prices; trend compares the latest price with the average

Correlation = Pearson correlation between the two daily return series

Data & sources

Analyses use the daily price history published on this site, not live market ticks.

Short windows give only an indicative result with lower reliability.
